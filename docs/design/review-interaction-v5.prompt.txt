Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: one refined interaction concept board for Think My Day, showing THREE states of the SAME minimal desktop companion interface.

Context: The user rejected four preceding concepts: generic office-software chat plus sidebar; cartoon mascots and illustrated scenery; pastel gradients and giant editorial greetings; then a terminal-style "geek" design. They want simplicity with a few thoughtful design ideas and a personal sense of companionship. Do not add new visual stereotypes. Explore quiet, content-led interaction design. This is a hypothesis for discussion, not a claim of an implemented product.

Main concept: "A conversation that quietly keeps your day together."
The clever design is that an ordinary conversation contains small time anchors that unfold into evidence; unfinished to-dos stay behind a tiny edge tab; a user's own clarification visibly becomes a supplementary note linked to that part of the day. The interface stays small, approachable and clean.

Board:
Landscape high-resolution, around 2400 x 1600 if possible.
Very pale neutral-gray background. At the top a small understated caption:
"Think My Day / 交互方向草图"
Below, three equal compact native-desktop windows arranged left to right with generous space between them, each about 30% of board width and 70% of board height. These are three states, not three alternative skins. All windows have exactly the same dimensions, typography and positions for shared controls.
Small headings ABOVE the windows, outside the app:
"01  先聊"
"02  顺手回看"
"03  补上一笔"
Small explanatory captions BELOW each window:
"记录不抢占界面"
"沿着一句话找到依据"
"你的补充成为当天的一部分"

Visual art direction:
An outstanding minimalist consumer app, contemporary, intimate and precise. Almost-white surfaces with warm graphite text, very restrained soft gray borders, one tiny muted burnt-orange accent used consistently for the active time anchor and supplement marker. No gradients, glow, dark mode, terminal syntax, monospace body text, fake code, avatars, mascots, illustrations, scenery, cards-within-cards, giant slogans, serif headlines or decorative paper. No dashboards, scores or productivity charts.
Normal highly legible Chinese sans-serif typography, compact but comfortable. Small native traffic lights, a subtle 12px-ish outer corner radius, a very soft realistic window shadow. Use alignment, affordances and state changes as the design. The app has a deliberately small human scale, like a personal companion you can open next to your work.
All Chinese exact copy must be accurate and readable.

Shared window structure:
A minimal titlebar reading "Think My Day", tiny traffic lights at left.
Under it, a plain date label "10月30日 · 今天", and a tiny right-edge tab reading "待办 2". This tab is a slim bookmark-like interface strip, not an illustration or paper ribbon. It can later open the to-do list; do not show a permanent right sidebar.
The main window is one continuous conversation with plenty of margins. AI text is directly on the white surface, no AI avatars, no repeated product labels, no speech-tail bubbles. The user's text has a subtly tinted gray inset with small rounded corners.
At the very bottom a modest 44px-ish height message input with placeholder "接着聊…" and a thin send-arrow icon; a tiny recording dot and "记录中" in the footer.
No giant greeting at the top. No conventional navigation sidebar. No permanent checklist consuming a third of the screen.

Window 01 ("先聊"):
AI text near top:
"下午，你花了一段时间对照储备资料。"
"这件事现在进展到哪里了？"
Below the message a small inline text anchor:
"14:08—14:26 · 对照资料"
The time is preceded by a tiny orange dash; at the right a delicate downward chevron indicates expandability. This looks like a footnote attached to a sentence, not a card or a button toolbar.
Lower down user text:
"报告还没写完，主要在核对数据。"
AI reply:
"哪一部分最难对齐？"
Leave real conversational breathing room.

Window 02 ("顺手回看"):
Same first AI message and same time anchor in the same positions.
The time anchor is now active in burnt orange and expanded. Immediately beneath it, show a compact inline evidence region defined by ONE thin left rule, not an enclosing heavy card:
"同时可见"
"左侧  Chrome · 储备报告"
"右侧  文档 · 研究笔记"
"正在操作：右侧文档"
Small quiet footer "来自窗口记录".
Keep it visually nested beneath the time anchor and local to the sentence.
Under the region, the same user text:
"报告还没写完，主要在核对数据。"
The conversation is still on screen. The interaction does not navigate to a separate log page. Input stays anchored to bottom.

Window 03 ("补上一笔"):
Same first AI text and date/tab structure.
The evidence region is collapsed again. The original time anchor still says:
"14:08—14:26 · 对照资料"
This preserves the source record.
User text:
"这段其实是在核对不同来源的储备数据。"
AI reply:
"明白，已经把这句补进今天的记录。"
Below the reply, a tiny orange dot and a compact supplementary line, in the same visual family as the original time anchor:
"用户补充 · 核对储备数据"
A smaller secondary phrase:
"关联 14:08—14:26"
This is an additional source-labelled note, not a replacement of the original captured facts. Do not add a checkmark or mark any to-do complete automatically.
Show a faint short linking line or aligned orange markers that visually connects the new supplement with the original time anchor. This subtle connection is the main clever detail; the screen remains clean.
Input stays at bottom in exactly the same position.

Constraints:
The visual should show a coherent thoughtful product interaction with little UI, not a marketing presentation with decorative arrows and giant paragraphs. Do not add any feature beyond the transcript, inline evidence, supplement note, small to-do tab, date and recording state. The user has not approved this interaction yet; this is a concept sketch with fictional content.
