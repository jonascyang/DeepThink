Use case: ui-mockup / style-transfer
Asset type: high-fidelity macOS UI design for Think My Day.

Input image 1 is the previous UI concept. The user now wants it "更加简约极客一点" (more minimal and geek-oriented). Completely redesign the visual style. Preserve only the product functions: a personal AI conversation about the day, user-controlled to-dos, date navigation, recording status, and end-day action.

Design direction: an exceptionally well-designed personal text terminal. Mature, spare, intimate, quiet. A developer would love its simplicity, but the Chinese conversation remains accessible and humane. Companionship comes from the actual dialogue, not mascots, slogans or decorative warmth.

Visual style:
- ONE clean macOS application window, straight-on, nearly filling a 1536 x 1024 landscape canvas. A small neutral dark margin. Flat screenshot, no hardware, no perspective.
- Warm near-black charcoal (#151514) main surface; pale off-white (#e6e5df) body text; readable warm gray secondary text. A single muted sage/olive accent (#a9b98a) for a tiny status dot, speaker label and prompt caret. Monochrome first, accent very sparingly.
- Crisp fine rules (#30302d), precise spacing, no visible gradients, no glow, no grain, no texture.
- SF Mono / IBM Plex Mono for Latin, time, numbers and labels, with a clean compatible Chinese sans-serif fallback. Visually consistent, compact, sharp Chinese text. NO serif typography.
- No mascots, avatars, illustrations, floating orbs, scenery, ornamental shapes, motivational headlines, giant greeting, cards, speech bubbles, dashboard panels, pill buttons, charts, graphs, progress meters or gamification.
- Native small traffic-light controls; small corner radius only on the outer window. Interior controls mostly text and fine square outlines.
- Outstanding restraint and alignment: an 8px rhythm, about 64px left margin, a comfortable 16–18px Chinese reading size, strong yet quiet hierarchy. Treat typography as the design.

Layout:
A thin 44px-ish titlebar contains traffic lights, small wordmark "Think My Day", and on the far right a tiny sage dot with "记录中", plain-text "结束一天", and a small understated settings icon.
Below, a narrow utility row contains previous/next chevrons and the date "2026-10-30 · 今天". Keep it subtle.
Main area: 74% width transcript on the left, 26% compact to-dos on the right, separated by ONE hairline vertical rule. It is not an enterprise sidebar: minimal label, plain-text rows, generous quiet empty space.
No additional navigation, rails, icon toolbars or analytics.

Quality bar:
A visually distinctive, implementable, lovingly precise developer-oriented consumer app. Strong text craft and disciplined simplicity, reminiscent of a beautifully configured personal terminal or plain-text editor, while remaining a welcoming conversation. Not neon-green hacker cosplay, not cyberpunk, not a generic gray office dashboard, not a marketing landing page.
All Chinese must be accurately rendered and readable. All visible activity and conversation are fictional sample data.
