Design brief:
A refined, contemporary, emotionally warm interface for adults. It should feel personal, attentive and quiet, like a beautifully designed private journal that talks with you. Accomplish companionship through typography, pacing, soft ambient color, a carefully chosen conversational voice and human-scale proportions. It must also have a confident visual identity and avoid the generic gray enterprise productivity app from the original iteration.

Visual direction:
- A single native macOS app window, straight-on, about 1440 x 900 within a 1536 x 1024 landscape canvas; a narrow clean neutral margin around it. No physical computer or perspective.
- Main surface is pale warm ivory with a very restrained luminous wash of dusty lilac and blush toward the upper-left corner. The effect is light passing through frosted glass, abstract and nearly flat, not an illustration.
- Warm deep-plum ink for headings; dark warm-gray body text; muted taupe secondary text; a restrained deep-plum action color.
- No characters, mascots, faces, pets, illustrated rooms, sunset scenes, furniture, paper books, paperclips, bookmark ribbons, skeuomorphic paper texture or 3D objects.
- A tiny abstract soft-halo identity mark, about 34 pixels, beside the conversational greeting: a beautifully subtle lilac/apricot blurred circle or overlapping translucent rings with NO FACE. This is a quiet presence, not a large hero decoration.
- Clear modern Chinese sans-serif body type, with one elegant slightly literary Chinese serif greeting. Crisp Chinese glyphs, comfortable body line-height and restrained scale. An art-directed consumer interface, precise but warm.
- The app should look buildable using real interface components, not a lifestyle illustration or a marketing landing page.

Composition:
Native tiny traffic-light controls in the top-left of a minimal titlebar. Small wordmark "Think My Day". At the upper-right, small quiet "记录中" with a subtle status dot, a low-emphasis "结束一天" control, and one settings icon.

The content is asymmetric: a spacious conversational area taking 72% width, a light small to-do area taking 28%. Separate them by whitespace, not a full-height boxed sidebar or a heavy vertical rule. No navigation rail, no dashboard tiles, no KPI cards, no charts.

Quality bar:
The user should notice sophisticated color, typography and space first; warmth and attention second; functional clarity third. Keep the composition clean and spacious but not empty. Do not solve the request simply by tinting an enterprise chat UI beige. Give the whole screen a cohesive plum/lilac/ivory visual signature with subtle asymmetry and expressive yet disciplined typography.
All visible Chinese must be accurate and legible. All activities are fictional sample data. No scoring, judgment, streaks, badges, cute language, cheerleading, upload confirmations or send logs.
